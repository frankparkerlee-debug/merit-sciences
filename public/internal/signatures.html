Merit — Team Email Signatures

Pick your team, fill in your details, hit Copy, then paste into your email client's signature editor.

Preview

Gmail: Settings → See all settings → Signature → paste (Cmd-V).
Apple Mail: Settings → Signatures → paste.
Outlook: Settings → Compose → Signatures → paste, or use “Copy HTML source” if your admin sets signatures centrally.
Images are hosted on meritsciences.com, so the signature stays intact for recipients.
